# Targets and Contacts

This is the draft target ledger for agencies and publishers that may fit *Lean and Agile*.

Status values:

- `researching`
- `promising`
- `hold`
- `not-target`
- `ready-to-approach`

Every entry should be re-validated before outreach.

## Priority Notes

- Major trade publishing will likely require an agent.
- Direct-submission publishers are still worth cultivating when the fit is strong.
- Submission requirements vary, sometimes a lot.

## Research Rules For New Targets

Before we add a new target as real outreach-ready, confirm:

1. the target is open now
2. the target handles business / leadership / organizational-change nonfiction
3. the target is traditional, not vanity or hybrid
4. the submission instructions are current
5. we know exactly what first-contact materials they want

## Contact Validation Notes

- Agent pages and publisher requirements can change often.
- We should verify each target again immediately before actual outreach.
- For agencies, the most important thing is not the office contact. It is the right agent plus the right submission path.
